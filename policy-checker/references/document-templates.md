the confirmed profile. Keep numbering clean and consistent.

## Form Consent Texts

- One exact checkbox text per consent purpose (marketing, profiling, data
  sharing, terms acceptance where needed).
- Format: short, specific, plain ("I agree to receive marketing emails from
  [COMPANY NAME] and understand I can unsubscribe at any time.").
- Rules: never pre-checked; separate checkbox per purpose (no bundling);
  essential-consent only where legally required (GDPR: no consent needed for
  contract performance).

## Email Footer / Unsubscribe

- Visible unsubscribe link (one click, no login, honored promptly).
- Physical or business address (CAN-SPAM), sender identity (LSSI), reason for
  email (opt-in reminder), preference center link (recommended).

## Legal Notice (Spain)

- Owner identity, contact, registration data (placeholder), hosting provider,
  purpose of the website, LSSI compliance statement.

## Age Gate / Minors Clause

- Minimum age statement, parental consent mechanism (if under-13/16 use is
  allowed and permitted), data minimization for minors, parental rights.

## AI/ML Disclosure

- What AI features exist, what data they process, whether data trains models,
  human review of automated decisions, opt-out route, Art. 22 GDPR rights.

## International Transfers Notice

- Where data is hosted, transfer mechanism (adequacy decision / SCCs / DPF),
  safeguard summary.

## DPA Summary (B2B)

- Roles (processor/controller), subprocessor list reference, security
  measures, breach notification timing, data deletion on termination,
  audit/cooperation clause. Note: full DPA should be a separate contract —
  this is the summary to publish.

## Accessibility Statement

- Conformance target (WCAG 2.1 AA / EN 301 549), known limitations, feedback
  channel, enforcement reference (ADA/EAA).

## Security Incident Notice

- Commitment to notify without undue delay, affected-party communication
  channels, regulator notification commitment (72h GDPR).

## Complaint Channel Notice

- Right to complain to the supervisory authority (name once known), internal
  complaint contact, response timeframe commitment.
